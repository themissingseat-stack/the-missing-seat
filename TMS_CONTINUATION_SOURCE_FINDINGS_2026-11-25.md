## Racial labeling of violence — measurement design
No full manuscript saved. Scope must distinguish:
- media framing: comparable incidents, story volume, racial labels, individualizing explanations, victim empathy;
- nonfatal victimization: victim survey definitions/denominators and uncertainty;
- homicide: separate fatality dataset and unknown-offender handling;
- arrest/conviction: enforcement/process measures, not every incident or innate group propensity;
