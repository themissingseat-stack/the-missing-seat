## Mayo: full paper inspected, not just a news summary
Faubion et al., DOI 10.1016/j.mayocp.2023.02.025:
https://internationalwim.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Impact-of-Menopause-Symptoms-on-Women-in-the-Workplace.pdf

Printed page 1 abstract confirms the four-site primary-care sample, ages 45–60, 2021 survey, 4,440 employed respondents, 13.4% reporting an adverse outcome and 10.8% missing work. It also states the low response and predominantly white sample. Printed page 3, Work Outcomes, defines self-reported absence over 12 months and reduced hours, layoff/firing, or quitting/retiring/changing jobs over six months, attributed by respondents to symptoms. Printed page 5 cost analysis extrapolates missed work to approximately $1.8 billion annually. The article properly identifies that number as the authors' extrapolation and does not turn the survey into proof of national employer causation. Both social versions retain survey attribution.
