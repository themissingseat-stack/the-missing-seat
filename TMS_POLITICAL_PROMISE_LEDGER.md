## PL-002: Household electricity bills and permitting

**Speaker/source:** Senator Shelley Moore Capito, November 9, 2026, [floor remarks published by the Senate committee majority](https://www.epw.senate.gov/public/index.cfm/press-releases-republican?ID=CCD27C16-CBA8-4E56-BFAE-C30D16E0AE61). Exact excerpt: "real wages will increase, electricity bills will fall, and everyday expenses will be reduced." She attributes those forecasts to the White House Council of Economic Advisers; they are not observed results or a personal guarantee. No household billing deadline in that passage.

**Action and constraints:** [November 10 motion to proceed passed 62 to 21](https://www.senate.gov/legislative/LIS/roll_call_votes/vote1192/vote_119_2_00258.htm). [November 11 Senate floor log](https://www.periodicalpress.senate.gov/wednesday-november-11-2026/) records cloture filings on substitute 6883 and H.R. 2347. The [November 12 schedule](https://www.democrats.senate.gov/2026/11/11/schedule-for-thursday-november-12-2026) lists 1 p.m. Washington time for first-degree amendment filings; the separately listed approximate 1 p.m. vote concerns a judicial nomination. Keep substitute, underlying vehicle, passage and actual law separate. Later measures of interest: allocation of grid costs and actual residential bills, with other rate drivers separated.

**Opposition and remaining burden:** [NCAI's statement](https://www.ncai.org/news/ncai-warns-baaja-s-5653) argues the revised measure still undermines tribal consultation and protection of sacred places. This is the organization's position, not a measured outcome. Compare operative text and actual consultation records before drawing a conclusion. No verified lower bills, consultation remedy or final passage in the sources checked at run time.

**November 13 check:** The [November 12 floor log](https://www.dailypress.senate.gov/thursday-november-12-2026/) schedules the substitute 6883 cloture vote after the Domenico nomination vote on November 16. The 5:30 p.m. Washington time agreement is when the motions ripen, not a promise of final passage. No change to household bills or tribal consultation is established.
