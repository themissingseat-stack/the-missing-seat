# TMS publishing control board — 2026-11-15

This board coordinates existing [editorial standards](TMS_EDITORIAL_STANDARD.md), [daily queue](TMS_DAILY_EDITORIAL_QUEUE.md), [story bank](TMS_STORY_BANK.md), [social ledger](TMS_SOCIAL_LEDGER.md), and [social copy](social/TMS_SOCIAL_TEASERS_2026-11-15.md). It does not override any of them.

## Production pipeline
1. **Lead** in story bank: source, country, significance, proposed angle, duplicate comparator, deadline.
2. **Research**: retrieve primary documents; distinguish fact, inference, contested claims; check novelty against published work.
3. **Full TMS Regular Joe Editorial Package**: complete natural-prose website article, receipts/links, six accountability questions, practical actions, Seat Summary, final thought, FB and IG texts by language, and metadata.
4. **Gate 1**: factual, legal, current-event, dates, links and source verification. A failed check means HOLD.
5. **Gate 2**: editorial, comprehension, voice, nonduplication, complete package, no artificial one-liners. Failed check means HOLD.
6. **Ready**: reserve one daily article slot. Before release, refresh time-sensitive claims and all functional links. Maximum one new article per day.
7. **Published** only after successful deployment and public URL/content verification. Merge is not publication.
8. **Social**: draft -> approved -> scheduled (Buffer confirmation) -> published (account verification). Do not infer one state from another. Germany stories require separate EN/DE Facebook and Instagram copy; US stories EN only. Instagram <=2,200 characters.
9. **Follow-up**: record outcomes, new evidence, corrections and meaningful updates; no duplicate editorials.
