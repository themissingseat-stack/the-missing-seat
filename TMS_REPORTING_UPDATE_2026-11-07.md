Takeaway/action: ask what a programme delivered, how it checked results and what support continues. Existing full package refreshed and published, not a new discovery of elimination.
2. **Coachman: the champion before the medal.** Seat: Black women's sporting skill and full-life recognition. [USATF](https://www.usatf.org/athlete-bios/alice-coachman), [Museum](https://usopm.org/alice-coachman/), [Albany State](https://asugoldenrams.com/news/2014/7/14/GEN_0714141255.aspx?path=general) and the [event record](https://www.olympedia.org/results/58812) give a richer account. Takeaway/action: teach performance beside milestone; check actual local participation costs before choosing support. New full approved package; queued.
3. **Cameroon: the treatment round that funding did not reach in time.** Seat: communities and health workers relying on predictable delivery. WHO's October report describes suspended external support and national money released too late for the planned 2025 round. The record is a report of past delivery, not evidence that all 2026 services stopped. Takeaway: a treatment donation still needs financing and logistics. Reporting action: obtain current government/partner schedules, disbursement and delivery records and frontline accounts. Research required, not a complete draft or verified new outcome. Same [authoritative report](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/who-wer10140-290-297); do not fold it into a claim that Niger lost its status.
