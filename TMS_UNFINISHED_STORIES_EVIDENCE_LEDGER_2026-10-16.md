- Current CMS implementation hub: https://www.medicaid.gov/resources-for-states/working-families-tax-cut-legislation/community-engagement

- State plaintiff's description of its challenge, a litigant position rather than a judicial finding: https://www.oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/attorney-general-bonta-sues-trump-administration-over-unlawful-implementation

CMS describes general January 1, 2027 implementation, specified covered adults, alternatives to paid work, exceptions, notice and an opportunity to demonstrate compliance. The article must not say everyone on Medicaid must work or that the nationwide implementation has already occurred.

Oz and Taylor v. Kennedy from the actual filings. The USDA primary memorandum returned 403 during retrieval. Secondary trackers locate the cases but do not replace the current orders. Do not imply the June lawsuit alone suspended the rule, or confuse separate noncitizen Medicaid litigation with work-requirement litigation.

Before completion: separate Medicaid and SNAP populations, implementation dates, exceptions and state administration in a claim ledger. Add policy-specific CBO projections and historical outcome research without treating projections as realized loss or past experiments as a current national result. Test the proposed removal-system thesis against participation, employment and procedural-loss evidence. No allegation of intentional harm without supporting evidence.
