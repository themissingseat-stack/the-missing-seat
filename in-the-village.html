In the Village

Life happens to everybody. Just not in the same way.

The Missing Seat looks at institutions, history and decisions. This section looks at the people living through ordinary life: how we think, feel, remember, misunderstand each other and try to make sense of things. Everyone has struggles. Money, discrimination, upbringing and circumstance change what those struggles cost.

It is a conversation, not advice. We are not here to tell anyone how to live. We are interested in why we live the way we do.

Three kinds of writing, three kinds of author

The label tells you who wrote it and how long it runs.

Village Note

A short text by Jansen or The Missing Seat.

An observation, a question or a small moment worth a few minutes. Published only when there is something to say.

Village Conversation

A longer piece by Jansen or The Missing Seat.

A subject that deserves room: how people cope, communicate, assume, change or stay the same. Claims that can be checked come with their sources.

Something Worth Sharing

A contribution from someone else.

Texts and contributions by other people live in their own section, credited to the person who wrote them.

What this is not

Not therapy, not self-help, not a diary.

Nothing here is a diagnosis or a treatment recommendation, and none of it replaces a professional. When we refer to research, we say what it found and how far it reaches. Personal experiences come from Jansen or from a contributor who is named or agreed to be anonymous. They are never invented, and no tool writes a life story for someone.

Understanding why someone behaved a certain way does not excuse harm. We try to hold both: curiosity about the reasons and clarity about the consequences.
