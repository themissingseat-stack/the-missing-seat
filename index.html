About The Missing Seat

The story changes when we ask who wasn't in the room.

The Missing Seat is an independent public-interest editorial platform built around a simple question: Who is missing?

We look beyond the loudest argument and examine the record, the people affected, who benefited, who carried the burden and what happened afterward.

We aren't interested in outrage for its own sake. We want readers to understand what happened well enough to decide what deserves their attention and what they can actually do next.

Why the chair?

Because power has a seating chart.

Decisions get made in government, schools, boardrooms, hospitals and neighborhoods. The people affected by those decisions aren't always represented when they are made.

Our chair is a reminder to ask who isn't there, whose evidence isn't being heard and who will live with the consequences.
