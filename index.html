PUBLIC-INTEREST PLATFORM

Who is missing?

The Missing Seat is a public-interest platform dedicated to one essential question: Who is missing from the conversation?

We uncover overlooked perspectives, publish evidence-based editorials, connect people with useful resources, and encourage meaningful action.

OUR MISSION

We bring overlooked stories and missing voices into the conversation.

Through verified storytelling, public awareness, and clear ways to act, we help people notice who is missing, understand why it matters, and discover meaningful ways to help pull up another chair.

Every story begins with one question: Who is missing?
Every story ends with one opportunity to help fill the seat.

ALLY = ACTION.

Caring is not the same as acting.

You do not need to lead every movement, speak on every issue, or have all the answers.

Start by noticing who is missing.

Listen before speaking. Learn before assuming. Speak when silence protects injustice. Support organizations already doing the work. Make room when you have access, influence, or opportunity.

One action will not change everything. But every empty seat filled changes something.

Choose one thing you can do today. Then do it.

This is only the beginning.

We are building a place where every story can lead to action: verified organizations to support, petitions worth signing, people to contact, resources to share, opportunities to volunteer, and practical ways to make room for someone whose seat is missing.

We will not ask you to do everything. We will help you find something you can do.

Every click counts. Every action matters. Every seat filled changes something.
