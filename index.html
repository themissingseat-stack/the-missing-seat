Our Story

Every empty chair has a story.

I started The Missing Seat because I kept noticing the same thing.

Some people are heard before they even speak. Others have to fight just to be noticed.

I saw it in workplaces, communities, politics, schools, and everyday life. People affected by decisions they had no voice in making. Stories that disappeared because something louder took their place. People expected to accept being overlooked and move on.

The Missing Seat began with a simple question:

Who is missing?

But asking the question is only the beginning.

We want to understand why people are missing, listen to the stories that are too easily ignored, and find practical ways to make room for more people at the table.

This is not about agreeing on everything.

It is about paying attention. Asking better questions. Learning from one another. And deciding that noticing something wrong should be the beginning of action—not the end of the conversation.

Every empty chair has a story. We intend to listen.

ALLY = ACTION.

Caring is not the same as acting.

You do not need to lead every movement, speak on every issue, or have all the answers.

Start by noticing who is missing.

Listen before speaking. Learn before assuming. Speak when silence protects injustice. Support organizations already doing the work. Make room when you have access, influence, or opportunity.

One action will not change everything. But every empty seat filled changes something.

Choose one thing you can do today. Then do it.

This is only the beginning.

We are building a place where every story can lead to action: verified organizations to support, petitions worth signing, people to contact, resources to share, opportunities to volunteer, and practical ways to make room for someone whose seat is missing.

We will not ask you to do everything. We will help you find something you can do.

Every click counts. Every action matters. Every seat filled changes something.
