VERIFIED RIGHTS UPDATE · 26 OCTOBER 2026

Four practical routes worth knowing now.

Each entry identifies the real rule, the relevant authority and the first usable step. A policy clarification is not necessarily a new law; a future program is not yet an open application route.

United StatesRestaurant & tipped workers · 19 October 2026

The federal 80/20 tip-credit guidance is no longer in force

Wage and Hour Division Bulletin 2026-1 withdrew three older opinion letters and confirmed that the 80/20 percentage guidance, already removed from its handbook in January 2025 after a 2024 court ruling, is not current enforcement guidance. The dual-jobs distinction remains: a genuinely separate non-tipped occupation cannot be paid using the tip credit. State rules may be stronger.

Record duties, hours, tips and cash wages · Check your state’s tipped wage rule · Ask Wage and Hour about the actual occupation and any underpayment

Official October 19 bulletin → State-by-state tipped wages → Wage complaint route →
United StatesTax refunds · 16 October 2026

Adoption credit: automatic refund, separate penalty review

The IRS is adjusting some 2025 returns with unused 2020–2024 adoption credits, generally without requiring amended returns. But it says an estimated-tax underpayment penalty will not automatically be recalculated as part of that adjustment. Not everyone is affected or entitled to a refund.

Keep the IRS adjustment notice · Compare it with your 2025 return · If an estimated-tax penalty might now be too high, follow the penalty notice or ask about Form 843

IRS official Q&A (Topic E, Q5) →
United StatesRetirement accounts · 22 October 2026

A new IRA error-correction route is coming — not open yet

The IRS announced an IRA Compliance Resolution System for certain inadvertent IRA failures and rollover issues, effective 1 May 2027. Existing no-fee automatic-waiver and self-certification procedures remain available to qualifying taxpayers now.

If a rollover went wrong, check existing waiver or self-certification options now · Do not assume the 2027 procedure is already available

IRS October 22 announcement →
GermanyEmployment ending · current statutory right

Arbeitsuchendmeldung: the employer also has responsibilities

Under §38 SGB III, people whose employment is ending generally must register as seeking work three months before the end, or within three days of learning about a shorter-notice end. §2 SGB III says employers should inform employees early and release them for relevant steps. The Federal Employment Agency confirms release for necessary appointments. Registering as seeking work is not the same as registering as unemployed.

Melde dich fristgerecht arbeitsuchend · Bitte um Freistellung für erforderliche Agenturtermine · Melde dich am ersten Tag der Arbeitslosigkeit zusätzlich arbeitslos

UNITED STATES

Rights people often discover too late.

United StatesRecords

Freedom of Information Act

Any person can request records from federal executive agencies, subject to statutory exemptions. If access is denied, check the agency's administrative appeal route.

Ask for the record behind the decision · Keep the request and response · Appeal when available

Official source →
United StatesLabor rights

Workers acting together

Many private-sector employees have federal rights to act together over wages, benefits and working conditions even when there is no existing union.

Document group concerns · Compare wages where lawful · Check NLRB coverage quickly if retaliation occurs

Official source →
United StatesWages

Minimum wage, overtime and retaliation

The Wage and Hour Division enforces federal wage-and-hour protections for covered workers. Keeping your own hours and pay records can matter.

Save pay stubs · Track hours · Check federal and state rules · Preserve retaliation evidence

Official source →

GERMANY

Rights that should not stay buried in the law book.

GermanySocial law

§14 SGB I: Right to advice

Everyone has a statutory right to advice about rights and obligations under the Social Code.

Ask: "Ich möchte Beratung über meine Rechte und Pflichten nach §14 SGB I."

Official source →
GermanyAdministrative rights

Akteneinsicht

If an administrative or social-law decision relies on information you have not seen, file-inspection rights may apply under the relevant procedural law.

Ask what is in the file · Request access where the statute applies · Keep the decision and Aktenzeichen

Betriebsrat and co-determination

Qualifying workplaces can elect a works council, which can have statutory participation rights in important workplace matters.

§84 BetrVG: Complaint right

Employees may complain when they believe they are disadvantaged, treated unfairly or otherwise adversely affected at work.

Document what happened · Use the formal complaint route · Involve the Betriebsrat where available

TMS will also keep tracking tax refunds, credits, unclaimed property, social benefits, housing support, family support and other money that can go untouched because people did not know they qualified, did not know where to apply, were not adequately informed or missed a deadline.

WHAT CAN I DO MONDAY MORNING?

Start with one problem, not the whole system.

  1. Write down exactly what happened and when.
  2. Find the right, law, benefit or procedure that may apply.
  3. Identify the person or institution with actual authority.
  4. Save the letter, pay stub, schedule, email, decision, case number or other evidence.
  5. Ask for important decisions and denials in writing.
  6. Check the deadline immediately.

A right you cannot use is barely a right at all.
