Take Your Seat · Get Involved

There’s more than one way to make a difference.

You don't have to be an activist, an expert, or a member of any organization to make something better. Maybe you have a story that needs telling. Maybe you want to understand your rights. Maybe you're looking for people already working on something you care about.

The Missing Seat is here to help people turn information into action—and make institutions answerable to the people they affect.

Have a story or question?

The Missing Seat investigates overlooked stories and makes useful information easier to understand. A secure story-submission channel is not yet available. Until one is established, please do not send confidential documents or sensitive personal information.

Newsletter and story-submission features will be added only when working systems, consent controls, and privacy information are in place. The Missing Seat does not currently offer individual legal representation or emergency assistance.
