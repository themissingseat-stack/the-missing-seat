Contact The Missing Seat

The Missing Seat helps turn information into action—and makes institutions answerable to the people they affect.

Write to themissingseat@gmail.com with a story suggestion, a correction, an offer of expertise or a question. A secure submission channel is not yet available, so email is for tips and questions only. Please do not send confidential documents or sensitive personal information.

The newsletter will open only when a working system, consent controls and privacy information are in place; subscriptions are not open yet. The Missing Seat does not offer individual legal representation or emergency assistance.
